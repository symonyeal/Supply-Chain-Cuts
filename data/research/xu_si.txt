Supplementary Figure 1: Stock dynamics model on the EV , battery, and material layers: 17 
research questions (left), model structure (m iddle), and included categories (right). 18 
Driving factors are classified into predominantly technical and socio-economic (in italic) 19 
drivers. We use two EC fleet development scenarios until 2030 from International Energy 20 
Agency (IEA)1. The EV range, fuel economy, and motor power of various EV models are 21 
collected from the US DOE (US Department of Energy) 2. The material compositions for 22 
various battery chemistries are calculated by using the BatPaC (Battery Performance and 23 
Cost) model from Argonne National Laboratory 3, expected for Li-Sulphur and Li-Air 24 
chemistries (marked with * as they are associated with uncertainty for EV applications 4). 25 
Abbreviations: E, B, and M = EV , battery, and material; e, b, and m = categories of EV , 26 
battery, and material; In, stk, and out = inflow, stock, and outflow; y = year. See section of 27 
battery replacement and reuse for calculation equations. 28

Supplementary Figure 12: Future battery waste stream by chemistry in the NCX (a), LFP 88 
(b) and Li-S/Air (c) scenarios until 2050 (unit: Mt = million tons). Under the STEP 89 
scenario, the EV battery waste stream reach around 11 Mt in 2050 if NCX scenario is 90 
realized, which is lower than the LFP scenario (14 Mt) and higher than the Li-S/Air 91 
scenario (9 Mt). The differences among thr ee battery chemistry scenarios are associated 92 
with the relatively lower specific energy of LFP chemistry and higher specific energy of 93 
Li-S and Li-Air chemistries compared to NC A and NCM series chemistries. Driven by 94 
higher EV fleet deployments, the weight of EV battery waste stream reaches around 2 times 95 
in the SD scenario than the STEP scenario. 96

Supplementary Note 1 434 
Average battery cell voltage = Average voltage of cathode – average voltage of anode. 435 
Relative to Li+/Li, the average voltage of graphite (360 mAh/g) and Si are 0.15 V and 436 
0.4 V . The average voltage of the graphite (Si) anode with 517 mAh/g active capacity 437 
can be estimated as (360/517)*0.15 + (157/517)*0.4 = 0.226 (V), based on 438 
assumption of a capacity averaged linear combination of graphite and Si 35. Therefore, 439 
compared to NCM622-Graphite, the open circuit voltage of NCM622-Graphite (Si) 440 
will be reduced by 0.226-0.15 = 0.076 (V)35. 441 
Supplementary Note 2 442 
The cell material compositions of Li-S batteries are obtained from 29, where required 443 
cell materials are scaled linearly by a factor between cell level energy capacity and 444 
required capacity of BEVs/PHEVs (see Supplementary Tables 2 and 3). The pack 445 
components of Li-S is calculated based on the pack configurations of default NCA 446 
chemistry in the BatPaC model3, that is to say the weight ratio of cell components and 447 
pack components, as well as the weight ratios of various components/materials in the 448 
pack configurations for Li-S are assumed equal to the NCA chemistry in the BatPaC 449 
model3. Similarly, we also use the same calculation methods for the material 450
