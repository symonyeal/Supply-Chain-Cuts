Recipe flexibility changes the failure scenario

Intervals describe Monte Carlo sampling uncertainty conditional on fixed inputs. They exclude input and model uncertainty. Recorded policy onsets did worse than a constant baseline out of sample, so these are what-if scenarios.

04 Inspect the evidence

Full research tables ↗

Each model node is a country producing one product. Production comes from output statistics; trade uses exports as a proxy; imputed includes conversions or material requirements. These bases can change the conclusions.

There is no located capacity for natural-hazard calibration. Export restrictions are policies, not measured shutdowns. The model covers neither prices nor investment returns.

What the model answers

Over 24 months, minimise unmet demand subject to capacities, material balances, inventories and spare-capacity ramps. Failure means a strictly larger shortfall than the threshold. Cuts contain at most three losses, lasting 3, 6 or 12 months.

The shared network weights batteries and solar equally after dividing each sector’s shortage by its own annual reference output. This is not combined GWh or economic value.

What the probability means

The exact lower bound L is the probability that at least one enumerated cut occurs under the saved policy-onset law. It is not a prediction of actual failure. U includes all states with more than three losses and can reach 100% without implying certain failure.

A Monte Carlo residual adds failures outside those cuts. Only reference configurations with saved runs display that estimate. Other settings show L and U alone.
