# v0.3.0 — 2026-10-17

Chemistry choice removes the Chinese precursor and refined-cobalt singleton cuts.
At unchanged reference capacities, five Chinese singletons and seven minimal cuts
replace seven and 37. Every former cobalt/precursor cut is restated; all 29 have
zero shortfall. This assumes manufacturing recipe flexibility, not measured ability
to switch actual plants. The approved 24-month share bounds are nonbinding on this
horizon, as proved in MODEL.

Under the unchanged full-outage onset law, Li-ion lower bound plus residual is
0.288286 (L = 0.288286, 95% sampling interval 0.288286–0.288471), versus 0.485681
for the fixed mix. Shared-network choice gives L = 0.105885 and estimate 0.106885
(0.106496–0.107429). These are conditional scenarios: the predictive test failed,
and legal onsets are not measured production outages. The residual estimator fixes
the earlier below-L importance-sampling point and retains a positive interval width
when zero residual failures are sampled.

The approved severity study freezes BACI 2012–2023 and the HS concordance. Only four
isolated events survive the declared rules; no measure reaches the five-event
minimum. S1/S2 are untestable. All severity assignments therefore use the declared
full-loss fallback; S3's five-point reduction is killed under that rule. This does
not show that actual policies remove full capacity.

New primary data are frozen with scope labels. METI Japan output, converted with
assumed voltage, removes one trade-dependent cell triple: six chemistry-choice cuts
remain and the same five singletons. The German sold-carbonate proxy changes no
headline cut. Comtrade partner batches reproduce preview rows, but overlap and
completeness remain unverified; one precursor row sum exceeds its BACI parent.
Four graphite lines return no rows, not zero trade. No qualifying Korean domestic
physical-output series was found. Raw data with unverified reuse terms stay local.

The preregistered competing-demand sensitivity keeps battery demand fixed. The
proportional Co/Ni reservation creates a 67.8% fixed-mix baseline shortage; flexible
chemistry meets demand at 10% Cu/Al/Mn availability. At 1% availability both recipes
fail without outages. These empty-cut certificates expose allocation assumptions;
they are not forecasts or measured industrial shortages.

All seven incomplete MILP checks now exhaust and exactly match their lattice lists.
A fresh independent reconstruction reproduces all 22 solar and 180 fixed-mix shared
cuts, with lower-bound differences below 3.3e-11. Independence limits and diagnosed
duration/HHI reporting differences are retained. The site shows shorter-duration
witnesses, shared controls, chemistry choice, sensitivity and capacity tables, and
explains the uninformative 100% upper bound.

Validation: 40 tests passed, including slow checks; lint and dependency checks pass.
All 128 frozen sources verify; all 52 committed source blobs match their hashes,
and the original 88 source hashes are unchanged. Mobile/desktop checks: 22, with
zero JavaScript exceptions or page overflow. Located-capacity numerators remain
zero across all 18 model layers; the 70% criterion fails, so natural hazards remain
country-wide scenarios. Administrative hazard matching and actual site losses
were not measured. BGS data remain unused pending licence clarification.
